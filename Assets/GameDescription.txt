GAME DESCRIPTION - CUBE-RAIL ENDLESS RUNNER (Unity)
THE MAIN GAME = Assets/MainScene prop.unity (URP, portrait, orthographic isometric)
Other scenes (MainScene, MainScene prop camera, TrialScene 1-4, TrialMonument,
TrialCircus, tutorial) share the same code; this file describes MainScene prop with
its actual serialized values. Values below are read from the scene file, not the
script defaults. Items marked [NONE FOUND] were searched for and are not in the project.

=====================================================================
1. ONE-LINE PITCH
=====================================================================
A small cube auto-runs along a procedurally generated, ever-turning chain of cubes
(Monument Valley look). The only input is LEFT / RIGHT tap: it rolls the runner around
the path onto another face of the cube chain, so it always stands on the face that
still has track ahead. Wrong face at a turn / bridge, or a red pyramid = death.
Endless, score-chasing, one restart tap.

=====================================================================
2. CORE MECHANICS & CONTROL LOGIC
=====================================================================
What a tap does (CubeRailRunner, Assets/PlayerController.cs)
- A tap ROLLS the runner 90 degrees around the path's long axis onto the neighbouring
  face (faces: 0 top, 1 right, 2 bottom, 3 left). Not lane change / steering / jump.
  The position swings on an arc round the cube edge while the body rotation is slerped
  (0.15 s), then re-seats on the new face. Single rolls have a 0.12 s cooldown.
- DOUBLE-TAP (same side twice within 0.25 s) = FLIP to the opposite face (180 deg,
  0.18 s, ignores cooldown, clears queue). Needed for boost zig-zags and some turns.
- Optional FLICK mode (ControlSettings, saved): swipe left/right rolls, swipe up/down
  flips, fires as soon as the finger moves 40 px. Default = tap / double-tap.
- Touch: swipe >= 50 px = that direction, otherwise tap left or right half of screen.
  Keyboard A/D or arrows. Inputs are buffered (max 5) and consumed in FixedUpdate so
  presses during a roll are never lost.
- Left/right are SCREEN-relative (cameraRelativeControls = on). The mapping is locked
  per leg: re-read from the camera only when the runner lands on a new heading
  (>= 30 deg) AND the camera has turned > 10 deg. A camera swing never flips controls
  mid-leg.

Movement / physics
- Rigidbody (no gravity, interpolated, continuous, constraints freeze rotation), sphere
  collider r 0.4, zero friction, path layer excluded from collisions. Every FixedUpdate
  velocity = forward along the cube (MoveSpeed) + a snap velocity to the target point on
  the current face (sideSwitchSpeed 30). So it is velocity-driven snapping, not forces.
- Two raycasts per physics step: forward probe (length scales with speed so a fast
  runner cannot skip a corner) finds the next cube; ground probe along the face's
  inward normal detects "no cube under me" -> isFlying.
- Path is fully procedural: DynamicPathGenerator (partial class across ~12 files) lays
  legs of cubes ahead and removes them behind, grouped in sealed ZONES of 2-3 legs
  (targetZoneSeconds 5, keeps 2 zones / 6 legs ahead, 5 zones behind). Turn choice
  weighted left 4 / right 4 / up 1 / down 1 with a re-randomised bias every 5 points and
  a 34% "standout" turn. Leg length 7-20 cubes, scaled with speed so a leg lasts about
  the same time at any speed.

Failure state
- Fall: off the path for 0.15 s = game over. Cause: standing on the wrong face at a
  turn, missing a corner, taking a decoy dead-end, or the wrong side of a bridge.
- Obstacle: red pyramid (PathObstacleGuard, trigger) = runner stops dead and shatters.
- It is easy to fail and purely skill-based: short fail window (0.15 s), speed keeps
  climbing to 4x, corner faces must be chosen ahead of time. There are no lives or
  revives; a run ends on the first mistake.

=====================================================================
3. OBSTACLES / HAZARDS / SPECIAL SECTIONS (MainScene prop values)
=====================================================================
- Corners: every turn requires the face that points toward the outgoing leg. Glowing
  yellow chevrons (TurnFaceArrows) are painted on that face at the corner end of the
  current leg and chase toward the corner; a yellow corner GEM sits in the notch of
  each sideways turn. Coins also sit on the correct face (see meta section).
- Red pyramid obstacle: from leg 10, 45% of legs, ONE pyramid (base 0.8 cube, height
  2.2) on the turn face near mid-leg (12% spread), 3-cube edge margin, yellow glow.
- Bridge legs: from leg 25, 30% of legs are laid 1.5x long; the middle is swapped for a
  slatted RED plank on ONE of the 4 faces (black outline). Standing on the wrong face
  when crossing = death (position-based BridgePlankGuard). 30% of bridges are
  multi-plank (1-3 planks, 1.4x more length, >= 4 plain cubes between). Bridge legs also
  get an arch decoration (BridgeArchDecoration). No coins on bridges.
- Decoy forks: from leg 20, 20% chance of a 3-4 cube dead-end stub off a corner,
  shaded like a real leg; taking it = fall.
- Boost sections: every 12-16 legs a 7-leg zig-zag of 12-cube legs that needs a
  double-tap flip each corner. Speed x1.2 (eased in over 0.25 s), camera zooms out to
  1.3x, apex coins, RunnerBoostFX.
- Portal zone change: every 30 legs the path runs into a flat red door (or, in this
  scene, a small glowing "level marker" cube with rays; useLevelMarker) via a 15-cube
  runway; must cross it on the TOP face (side 0). World retints red as you cross
  (PortalZoneController), bonus coins, camera yaw +45.

=====================================================================
4. CAMERA & PERSPECTIVE
=====================================================================
- Strict orthographic isometric: tilt 35.264 deg, yaw offset 45. Main Camera ortho size
  22 (authored floor: minOrthoSize 22, never zoom below), boom distance 60.
- CameraParentRotator (on CameraParent) is a state machine: Static / Transitioning.
  The generator plans a yaw per zone (PlanCameraYaws, dynamic programming with
  angleChangeCost 11, yaw quantised to 90 deg steps) so the whole zone is framed
  (framingPadding 3). Entering a new zone triggers a glide (1.2 s, shortened to
  match runner speed, min 0.6 s; ~0.7 s per 90 deg), held at least 2.5 s between shots.
- Follow: followPlayer 0.45, followSmoothTime 0.15, look-ahead 10-20 cubes and 1
  corner, soft/hard frame limit 0.85/0.88 keeps the runner in view, zoom fits the
  visible track with smoothing (0.5 s, tighten 6/s).
- Reacts to state: slow in-place lean/sway (5 deg, 0.8 s smoothing); boost zoom-out
  x1.3 (in 0.5 s, out 0.25 s); on death the camera follows the fall arc ~0.2 s, then
  FREEZES to watch the shatter, then rewinds (0.4 s ease-out) on restart.
- NOT active in this scene: speed-based zoom (speedZoomEnabled 0), flip-impact
  shake/punch (flipImpactEnabled 0; shakeMagnitude 0.4/0.15 s and punchAmount 1.5 exist
  but are gated off). No FOV effects (orthographic).

=====================================================================
5. ATMOSPHERE, ENVIRONMENT & SHADERS
=====================================================================
- Art: low-poly Monument Valley look. Blue path cubes, pale sky, soft shadows, warm
  yellow accents (gems, chevrons, coins), RED for danger (planks, pyramids, portal).
- Scene contents: MonumentEnvironment + MonumentValleyScene (hand-placed tower of cubes
  with yellow TOTEM blocks: SDF-drawn eye / maze / paths / rivets, 3D eye + rivet discs),
  SoftSun directional light, MonumentShadowFloor, MonumentSkyBackdrop.
- Custom shaders (Assets/TrialMonument/Shaders + Assets/Special Materials):
  TotemBlock (unlit URP SDF designs, flat per-face tints, contact-shadow circles; also
  used for the red pyramid), MonumentBlock, MonumentSky, MonumentShadowFloor, Portal
  Block/Ray/Void/Overlay.
- Route readability without perspective: PathDepthShading darkens/fades cubes per cube
  by distance ahead along the route; ActiveFaceHighlight draws a lit lane on the face the
  runner is on and flashes with a sweeping band when you switch face.
- Post-processing: a PostProcessVolume (global) exists but its profile
  (TrialScene_PostProcess) has NO effects. So no bloom / vignette / colour grading /
  AO currently. Glows are fake (additive quads, shader tricks).
- Environment: the path is procedural and rebuilt on the fly; background props in this
  scene are hand-placed (no procedural houses in this scene). Path cubes are clones of
  a single cube prefab (firstCube / cubePrefab).

=====================================================================
6. GAME FEEL & JUICE (what actually exists)
=====================================================================
On tap
- Haptics.LightImpact() per roll.
- Body arc + rotation roll; ActiveFaceHighlight flash + sweep band on the new face.
- RunnerTrailFX: restyled ribbon trail, wisps, spark motes, shockwave rings on
  gameplay events (colours shift with speed). Never touch trail.time.
- Tap / roll SOUND [NONE FOUND]. No squash & stretch [NONE FOUND].

Success communication
- Coins: spinning glowing coins with blob shadow, picked up by proximity radius 0.7
  (no colliders). Counter in HUD. Coin pickup SOUND [NONE FOUND].
- Corner gems, chevrons, guide-lane on correct face; portal + level marker as
  milestone; boost zig-zag with camera zoom-out is the main "flow" moment.
- No score popups, combo, or perfect-turn reward. Audio: [NONE FOUND] beyond the fall
  chime (no music, no coin/boost/portal sounds).

Failure communication (GameOverSequence)
- Runner leaves the path with its momentum, gravity on, sails off in an arc; the next
  cubes of the missed route light up as GHOST cubes (shows what you should have done)
  then breathe; camera locks at t=0.2 s; at t=0.3 s the runner shatters into pooled
  shards; synthesised "stone dropped down a well" falling-sine chime with echoes.
  Obstacle hit = stop dead, shatter in place, shards keep impact direction.
- HUD (RunnerHUD, built in code): score top-left, coin counter, then game-over panel
  with big final score, BEST / "NEW BEST", a "near miss" line when within 90% of best,
  and a tap-to-restart prompt. Soft restart (~1-2 s): path sinks, new one rises,
  camera rewinds; no scene reload.

=====================================================================
7. PROGRESSION & PACING
=====================================================================
- Endless score-chaser. No levels, no lives, no checkpoints. Portal every 30 legs is a
  visual/zone milestone, not a level.
- Score = coins/points; speed rises in STEPS on score (steppedSpeed): start 11.2, every
  5-10 points speed += 1.05 (step grows x1.2), ceiling 10x cap logic (maxSpeed starts
  47.6, raised by generator). Leg length also grows (pathGrowth 1.2, max 35 cubes) so
  reaction time stays comparable while corners arrive faster.
- Difficulty gates by leg: obstacle pyramid from leg 10, decoy forks from leg 20,
  bridges from leg 25, boost zig-zag every 12-16 legs, portal every 30.
- Turn bias re-randomised every 5 points; 34% standout turns; leftWeight = rightWeight.
- Coin lines: 8-15 per run of coins, 50% spawn chance, 70% chance a coin run starts on
  the WRONG face (temptation lure, smaller coins) - risk/reward.

=====================================================================
8. RETENTION & META-GAME
=====================================================================
Present
- Best score saved in PlayerPrefs (key RunnerBest_<sceneName>), "NEW BEST" and
  "near miss" (>= 90% of best) messaging on the game-over screen.
- Frictionless one-tap restart with an animated soft reset = "one more run".
- Control mode (tap/double-tap vs flick) saved. One-time tutorial scene.
- Coins collected and shown per run.

Not present [NONE FOUND]
- Persistent currency, shop, skins/trails unlocks, daily rewards / streaks, missions,
  achievements, leaderboards, ads / IAP, revive, music, settings menu beyond control
  mode, analytics. Coins reset on restart (CoinCounterUI.ResetCount).
